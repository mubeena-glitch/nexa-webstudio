Tambala Valley, harvest season
Meet Esi

The grandmother who reforested a hillside.

When the rains stopped coming, Esi planted forty seedlings behind her home. Today that hillside is a 12-hectare forest, and forty other families have followed her lead.

“I won't see these trees grow tall. But my granddaughter will sit in their shade.”

Esi Mensah — Community lead, Tambala Valley
