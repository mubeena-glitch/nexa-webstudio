Our story

A kitchen built around the hearth

Ember began with a single wood-fired oven and a simple belief: that the best meals are unhurried, generous, and shared. Every dish that leaves our pass passes through smoke and flame first.

We work hand-in-hand with growers within a day's drive of the city, letting the season write the menu. What arrives at the back door each morning decides what you'll taste that night — nothing more, nothing less.

Marco Vento Chef & Founder · Ember Wood-Fired Kitchen
