Plain light. Real objects. No fuss.

I spent six years assisting food photographers in London before opening my own studio in Manchester in 2021. I now shoot for about twenty brands a year, most of them small and all of them making something by hand.

I work with one light and real props wherever possible, because the photo should look like the thing you will unpack. I art direct my own shoots, so there is one person to brief and one person to blame.
