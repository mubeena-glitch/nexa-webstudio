Our House

A merchant's house, kept honest

Built in 1788 and lovingly held by the same family for three decades, Rosewood was never meant to be a grand hotel. It is a home that happens to take guests.

We restored what we could and replaced only what we had to. The floors creak in the right places. The brass is unpolished on purpose. Breakfast is whenever you wake.

Eleanor & James Whitfield, owners
