03 / Process

A typical identity takes fourteen weeks.

Listen

Interviews with your team and customers, and a walk round wherever the product is made.

Weeks 1 to 3

Draft

Two routes, shown on real things: a box, a homepage, a van. Never on a mood board alone.

Weeks 4 to 7

Build

The chosen route becomes a system: type, colour, grids and templates, tested in use.

Weeks 8 to 12

Hand over

Guidelines, files and a half-day session with whoever will look after it next.

Weeks 13 to 14
