I photograph the hour that most people miss. The thin, uncertain light at the edge of a day, when a landscape stops performing and simply is.

Working almost entirely in available light, I move slowly and shoot sparingly. A single frame can take a morning of waiting, and that patience is the whole point. Nothing here is staged and nothing is rushed.

Based on the west coast of Scotland, I travel for commissions across Europe and the far north, building long, quiet bodies of work for editorial clients, galleries and private collectors.
