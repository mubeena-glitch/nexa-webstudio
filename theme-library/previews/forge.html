How we work

From enquiry to delivery, in four controlled stages

A single project engineer owns your job from quote to dispatch, so you always know who to call.

STAGE 01

Quote & review

We assess drawings, materials, and tolerances, then return a fixed quote with a clear lead time.

STAGE 02

Plan & programme

CAM programming, fixturing, and a first-article plan are signed off before any metal is cut.

STAGE 03

Make & inspect

Parts are machined, welded, and finished in-house, with inspection built into every stage.

STAGE 04

Certify & ship

Final inspection, full documentation, and dispatch, packed to protect critical surfaces.
