A house built on the conviction that restraint is the truest form of luxury — that what is left unsaid speaks loudest.

Founded in Paris in 1987, Noir has spent four decades refining a single idea: clothing as quiet authority. Each piece begins in our atelier, where master tailors work in materials sourced from a handful of trusted houses.

We release two collections a year. Nothing more. Each garment is made to be kept — and to be passed on.
