What we do, and how far we take it

From a single room to a full home, we work at whatever depth suits the brief. Each engagement is scoped honestly and priced before we begin.

01 Full interior design Concept to completion: spatial planning, materials, joinery, lighting, and the final styling.
02 Renovation & build We manage trades, drawings, and the programme so the finished space matches the visual exactly.
03 Furniture & styling Sourcing, commissioning, and arranging the pieces that make a room feel finished, never staged.
04 Design consultation A focused session and a clear written direction for those who want to carry out the work themselves.

How we work

Four steps, no surprises

Phase 01

Listen

We walk the space, learn how you live, and gather the references that already speak to you.

Phase 02

Shape

Plans, materials, and a clear visual direction, presented as one considered scheme rather than endless options.

Phase 03

Make

We manage the trades and the timeline, keeping you informed without making it your second job.

Phase 04

Settle

Styling, the final details, and a handover so the space feels yours from the first evening.
