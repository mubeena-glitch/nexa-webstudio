Our story

A corner bakery run by people who love bread

Marigold started as a market stall with one borrowed oven. A decade later we are still a small team, still mill our own flour, and still believe a good loaf is worth the wait. Nothing here is rushed and nothing here is mass made.

  • Flour milled in-house from regional grain
  • Slow ferments, no shortcuts, no additives
  • Leftover bread donated to the local shelter
