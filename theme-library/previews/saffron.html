Our kitchen

A grandmother’s recipes, an exacting hand

Saffron began as a single clay tandoor and a handful of family recipes carried from Lucknow to London.

Our chef patron grinds every masala fresh each morning, toasting whole spices to order so no two services taste quite the same. The kitchen sources its lamb from the Welsh hills, its prawns from Cornish day boats, and its saffron, of course, from Kashmir.

What arrives at your table is neither nostalgia nor reinvention. It is the food of a home kitchen, cooked with the discipline of a great one, and served the way it was always meant to be shared.
