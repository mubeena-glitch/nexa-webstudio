Our approach

Therapy that meets you where you are

No jargon and no pressure. Just a steady, collaborative space to understand what you are carrying and to find a lighter way forward.

Warm and unhurried

Every session is yours. We listen first, set the pace together, and never rush you towards a tidy conclusion.

Evidence-based

Our therapists draw on CBT, ACT, and compassion-focused methods, chosen to fit you rather than the other way round.

Genuinely yours

We match you with a therapist you click with, and you can change at any time. The relationship is what does the work.

Why Sage

A practice built around feeling safe enough to be honest

We started Sage because good therapy should feel human, not clinical. Comfortable rooms, therapists who genuinely listen, and a clear path so you always know what to expect next.

  • Free 20-minute introductory call before you commit
  • Evening and weekend appointments available
  • Online and in-person, whichever feels right
