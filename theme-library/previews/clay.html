Our hands

Made slowly, the way it has always been made

We centre, pull and trim every piece on the wheel. There is no mould and no machine. A mug takes the time it takes, and we think you can feel that when you hold one.

  • Local stoneware clay, dug and reclaimed in-house
  • Glazes mixed from raw materials, never bought ready-made
  • Fired in our own kiln, twice, to 1,260 degrees
Open studio every Saturday
Built to last

Pieces that earn their place on the table

Our tableware is dishwasher safe, microwave safe and made to be used daily. We would rather make something you reach for every morning than something you keep behind glass.

  • Food-safe, lead-free glazes throughout
  • Repairs and re-glazing offered on every piece
  • Plastic-free packing, posted in recycled board
From clay to cupboard

Four hands-on stages, no shortcuts

Each piece passes through the same patient sequence before it ever leaves the studio.

01

Throwing

Soft clay is centred and pulled on the wheel into its rough living shape.

02

Trimming

Once leather-hard, the foot is turned and handles are pulled and joined.

03

Glazing

After a first firing, each piece is dipped in glazes mixed by hand that week.

04

Firing

A final kiln firing to stoneware temperature sets the glaze for life.
