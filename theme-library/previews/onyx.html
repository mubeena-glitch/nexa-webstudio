A house founded on a single conviction: that true luxury is measured slowly, finished by hand, and built to outlast its maker.

Established in Geneva in 1908, Onyx has spent more than a century perfecting the mechanical watch and the art of the setting. Every movement is assembled, regulated and signed by a single watchmaker.

We produce a limited number of pieces each year. Nothing is rushed. Each timepiece carries a guarantee for life, and a service promise that passes to the next owner.
