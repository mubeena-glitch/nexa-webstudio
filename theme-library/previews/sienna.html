Interview

"We were never in a hurry, and it shows."

Imogen Hart bought the barn in 2015 with a mortgage, a kiln and no plan. The roof leaked, the floor was earth, and the nearest neighbour kept sheep in what is now the kitchen.

Rather than renovate in one go, she and her partner Tom lived in a single room for three years, finishing each space only when they knew how they wanted to use it.

"Every room was finished by the life we lived in it, not by a drawing."Imogen Hart, ceramicist

Column: At the table

Bread, butter and the art of doing less

Every winter I make the same promise: fewer dishes, better ones. This year it finally stuck, mostly because my oven broke in October and I spent three weeks cooking on two rings.

What survived was a loaf I could make half asleep, a jar of cultured butter, and a salad that changes with whatever the market has left by Saturday afternoon.

Ama OwusuFood editor, writing from Hackney
