Questions

The things release managers ask first.

Does Halden need access to our source code?

No. Halden works from store metadata, build artefacts and the monitoring tools you connect. It never reads your repository.

What happens if a health gate fails at night?

The rollout pauses at its current percentage and the on-call owner is paged through PagerDuty or Opsgenie. Nothing expands until a person says so.

Can we use it for React Native and Flutter apps?

Yes. Halden manages releases at the store level, so any framework that ships to the App Store or Google Play is supported.

How long does migration from a spreadsheet take?

Most teams import their checklist and calendar in under an hour. We will do it for you on the Team plan.
