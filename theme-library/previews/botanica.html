New plant, no panic

Four small habits keep them happy

Most houseplants fail from kindness, not neglect. Follow these and you will rarely lose one.

Find the light

Read the care card and place your plant where its leaves get the brightness it likes, without harsh midday sun.

Water by weight

Lift the pot. Light means thirsty, heavy means wait. Most plants prefer to dry out a little between drinks.

Feed in season

From spring to early autumn, a diluted feed every few weeks keeps growth steady and leaves a deep green.

Repot in time

When roots circle the base, size up by one pot. Bring it to our Sunday clinic and we will do it with you.
