Questions before you send photos

What if the job turns out bigger than the photos?

The technician shows you on site and sends a revised price before doing anything extra. You can say no and pay only for what was quoted.

Do you do plumbing and electrics?

Small fixtures only: taps, shower heads, light fittings, sockets and switches. Rewiring, water heaters and new circuits go to our licensed partner, and we tell you up front.

Can you work on a Friday or Saturday?

Yes. Weekend slots cost AED 40 extra per visit.

Do you supply paint and materials?

Yes, at the shop price with no mark-up. We use Jotun and National Paints, and the receipt goes with your invoice.

December slots are booking about four days ahead.
