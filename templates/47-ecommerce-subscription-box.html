How it works

01

Tell us how you brew

Cafetière, V60, AeroPress, moka pot, espresso or whole bean. We grind to suit on the day we pack.

02

We roast on Tuesday

Small batches on our 15 kilo Diedrich, in a unit off Newhaven Road.

03

It arrives Thursday or Friday

Letterbox-size, Royal Mail tracked. You do not need to be in.

04

Rate it in the app

Thumbs up or down on each origin. We use it to steer what you get next.

Bought direct, priced openly

We buy from five importers and two farms directly, and print what we paid per kilo on every bag. This season our average green coffee price is £8.94 a kilo, about three times the commodity price.

In every delivery

  • Coffee roasted no more than 72 hours before dispatch
  • A card with the farm, variety, process and the price we paid
  • A brew recipe for your method, by our head roaster Eilidh Drummond
  • Packaging that goes in your paper recycling, and a compostable bag liner
  • A code for 15% off anything in the online shop
