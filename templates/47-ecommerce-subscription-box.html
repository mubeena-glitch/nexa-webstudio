FAQ

Before you sign up

How much is a subscription?

A 250g box is nine pounds a fortnight including delivery, and your first box is half price. Bigger 500g and 1kg plans are available if you get through more.

Can I choose the coffee?

Each fortnight we surprise you with a new single origin matched to your taste profile. Prefer to drive? Switch to our pick-your-own plan any time from your account.

How does delivery work?

Boxes are designed to fit through a standard UK letterbox, so you do not need to be home. Free first-class delivery on every plan.

What if I want to cancel?

Cancel, pause or skip from your account in two taps. No minimum term, no cancellation fee, no awkward phone call.
