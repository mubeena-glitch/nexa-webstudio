FAQ

Before your visit

How far in advance can I book?

Reservations open two weeks ahead. Tuesday at noon for the following two weeks.

Do you do private dining?

The chef's counter (six seats) is available for private buyout. Minimum spend applies.

Dietary requirements?

Tell us at booking. We do not list allergens publicly but we always accommodate. Tasting menu is fully vegan on request.

Children?

Welcome up to 8pm. Smaller portions on request. After 8pm, the room becomes adults only.
