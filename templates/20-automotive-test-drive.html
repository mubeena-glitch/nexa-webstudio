Before your test drive

Can you bring the car to me?

Yes. Home and office test drives are free within Dubai and Abu Dhabi city limits, booked 48 hours ahead.

Can my family come along?

Yes. In the X6 there is room for five passengers plus the specialist.

Do you take trade-ins?

Yes. Bring your car on the day and we value it in 20 minutes. The offer is valid for seven days.

How long is the wait for delivery?

S4 in white, grey and black is in stock in Dubai. Other colours and the X6 take six to nine weeks.

What finance is available?

From 2.49% flat over 60 months with 20% down payment, through two UAE partner banks. Subject to approval.
