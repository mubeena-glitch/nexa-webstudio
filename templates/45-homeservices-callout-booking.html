Before we arrive

What does the AED 149 cover?

The visit, up to 30 minutes on site and a written diagnosis. If we fix it within that time, you pay for parts only.

What should I do while I wait?

For leaks, turn off the main stopcock, usually under the kitchen sink or by the water meter. For electrical faults, switch off the main breaker and do not touch wet sockets.

Do you work for tenants?

Yes. We send the invoice and photos in a format most landlords and property managers accept for reimbursement.

How do I pay?

Card, Apple Pay or bank transfer on completion. No cash needed.

Are your technicians qualified?

Every electrician is DEWA approved, and every plumber has at least five years of residential experience in the UAE.
