What it does

Rent by room

The master bedroom pays more than the box room. Set the split once, it repeats every month.

DEWA and ADDC bills

Snap the bill, Rotapay reads the total and divides it by days each person was home.

Internet and streaming

Shared subscriptions logged once, split automatically when the renewal date comes round.

Chores that rotate

Bins, bathroom, kitchen. The rota moves on each Sunday and skips whoever is travelling.

Settle up in one tap

See one net figure per person, then pay by bank transfer. No more chains of small IOUs.

Move-out made simple

When someone leaves, Rotapay shows exactly what they owe up to their last night.

Questions

Does Rotapay hold our money?

No. You pay each other by your usual bank transfer. Rotapay keeps the record and marks payments as settled.

Do all my housemates need the app?

They need an invite link to see their balance. Anyone without a phone handy can view it in a browser.

Which cities are in the beta?

Dubai, Sharjah and Abu Dhabi. Bill reading works for DEWA, SEWA and ADDC bills.

What happens to our data?

It is stored on servers in the UAE. We never sell it, and you can delete your household in settings.
