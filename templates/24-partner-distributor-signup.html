Before you apply

Who is the programme open to?

Distributors and resellers with proven coverage in the MEA region. Minimum two years of operational history.

What is the minimum order quantity?

Entry tier: AED 40,000 quarterly. Mid tier: AED 120,000. Top tier: AED 350,000.

How long is the application process?

Submit application, sales call within 5 days, contract within 11 days, first shipment within 21 days.

Are there exclusive territories?

Top-tier partners can negotiate territory exclusivity at annual review. Entry and mid tiers are non-exclusive.
