Before you apply

Do we need cold chain?

No. The cans are shelf-stable at ambient temperature. Chilling is only needed at the point of sale.

Who pays for registration in our market?

Qamar holds the product registrations. You handle import permits as the importer of record.

Can we cover more than one country?

Yes, if you already distribute there. Each country is reviewed separately.

What if we miss the volume target?

You stay on the launch tier. Exclusivity is only reviewed if volume falls below 1,800 cases a year.
