Questions about the first batch

Why only 340?

That is what four people can turn, assemble and calibrate properly in eight weeks. We would rather sell out than rush the burr alignment.

Is joining the list a commitment?

No. You can ignore your order window and nothing happens.

Can it grind for espresso?

Yes. Settings 4 to 11 cover espresso. It works best on flat-bottom and bottomless baskets.

What if it arrives damaged or I change my mind?

You have 30 days to return it unused for a full refund. Damage in transit is replaced from a reserve of 12 units.

Do you ship outside the GCC?

Not for batch one. The UK and Europe are planned for batch two.
