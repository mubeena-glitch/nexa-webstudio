FAQ

Before your visit

What should I bring?

Just yourself. We provide robes, slippers, towels and a locker. Swimwear is needed for the thermal suite, and you are welcome to bring your own.

How early should I arrive?

We suggest arriving forty-five minutes before your treatment so you can settle into the thermal suite first. Late arrivals may shorten your treatment time.

How do gift vouchers work?

Choose a treatment or a spa day, add a message, and we send the voucher by email or post. It is valid for twelve months and can be redeemed against anything on the menu.

What is your cancellation policy?

Reschedule or cancel free of charge up to forty-eight hours before your appointment. Inside that window we ask for fifty per cent of the treatment cost.
