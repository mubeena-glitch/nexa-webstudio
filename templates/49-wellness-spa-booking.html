From guests

Your visit

How early should I arrive?

Thirty minutes before your treatment, so you have time in the steam room and plunge pool first.

What should I bring?

Nothing. Disposable underwear is provided for the hammam and body treatments. Swimwear is optional in the plunge pool.

Can I cancel or move my booking?

Free up to 24 hours before. Inside 24 hours we charge 50% of the treatment price.

Is there parking?

Yes, two free bays behind the villa, entered from 15A Street. Otherwise RTA paid parking on Al Wasl Road.

The garden is quiet on weekday mornings

Book a treatment and come early for the steam room.
