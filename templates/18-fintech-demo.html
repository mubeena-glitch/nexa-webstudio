Questions finance teams ask us

Is Tadfuq a bank?

No. We are a software platform. Cards are issued by our partner bank, and your money stays in your own accounts.

How is our data secured?

SOC 2 Type II audited, data hosted in the UAE, read-only bank connections unless you enable payments.

What does it cost?

Plans start at AED 3,900 a month for up to five entities, with cards priced per active card. You get an exact quote after the demo.

Does it work with our ERP?

Yes for Oracle NetSuite, Microsoft Dynamics 365, SAP Business One, Odoo and Zoho Books.

What if we only want treasury, not cards?

That is fine. Around a third of customers start with treasury and add cards later.
