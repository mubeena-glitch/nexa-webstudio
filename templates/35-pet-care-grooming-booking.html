What owners tell us

Before you book

Do my pets need vaccinations?

Yes. Dogs need up-to-date rabies and DHPPi, cats need rabies and tricat. Upload the card when you book, or show it on arrival.

My dog is anxious or reactive. Can you help?

Tick the box when you book and we will give you the first slot of the day, when the salon is quiet, and allow an extra 30 minutes at no charge.

Do you use sedation?

Never. If a groom is not safe without it, we stop, tell you why and suggest a vet groom.

How often should I book?

Most doodles and poodle crosses need a full groom every 5 to 6 weeks. Short coats are fine with a bath and tidy every 6 to 8 weeks.

What if I need to cancel?

Free up to 24 hours before. Inside 24 hours we charge AED 50, or the van call-out fee.
