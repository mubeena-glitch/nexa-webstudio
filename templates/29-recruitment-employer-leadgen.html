Before you brief us

Do you work on exclusive or contingent terms?

Both. Exclusive briefs get a shortlist in 9 working days. Contingent briefs take longer because we prioritise exclusive work.

Is there a retainer?

Only for searches above AED 70,000 a month base salary, where a third of the fee is paid at the start.

Which countries do you cover?

UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Bahrain, Kuwait and Oman. Most of our placements are in Dubai, Abu Dhabi and Riyadh.

Can you hire contractors?

Yes, for finance and data roles of 3 to 12 months, on our payroll.
