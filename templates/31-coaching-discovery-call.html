The promotion came with a team twice the size and a boss who expects you to already know how. Most of my clients are not struggling. They are busy, capable and quietly unsure which of their old habits still serve them.

How the twelve weeks run

Six 75-minute sessions, one structured feedback round and a written plan you keep.

01

Weeks 1 and 2: the brief

A long first session on what the role actually asks of you. Then I interview eight to ten colleagues you choose and write up a confidential 360.

02

Weeks 3 to 8: the work

Fortnightly sessions on the two or three patterns the 360 surfaced. Between sessions, one small experiment each time, reviewed in a 10-minute voice note.

03

Week 9: a three-way

If your organisation is sponsoring, we bring your manager in for 45 minutes to agree what progress should look like to them.

04

Weeks 10 to 12: the handover

Two final sessions and a four-page leadership plan for the next six months. A check-in call at week 20 is included.

Questions people ask on the discovery call

Is the discovery call a sales call?

No. I ask what has changed in your role and what you want to be different by March. If coaching is not the right tool, I will say so and suggest what might be.

Can my company pay for it?

Yes, and most do. I send a one-page proposal with objectives, schedule and fee for your HR or L&D team. What we discuss stays between us; your sponsor only joins the week 9 meeting.

Online or in person?

Either. About two thirds of sessions run on Zoom. In-person sessions are at our room in Gate Village 4, DIFC, with parking at Gate Avenue.

What if I travel a lot?

Sessions can move with 48 hours notice, and the programme can stretch to 16 weeks if your quarter is heavy.

When are the next start dates?

I take four new clients a month. There are places starting 7 December 2026 and 11 January 2027.
