How the morning runs

Most of the 4.5 hours is spent on the floor, not in a chair.

01

8.30am: scene safety and adult CPR

Compressions on QCPR manikins that show your depth and rate live. You keep going until you hit 100 to 120 a minute at 5 to 6 cm.

02

10am: AED, bag-mask and two-rescuer CPR

You run the AED and bag-mask in pairs, then switch roles. Every learner leads at least two scenarios.

03

11.15am: child, infant and choking

Infant compressions, two-thumb technique and relief of choking for all ages.

04

12.15pm: skills test and written exam

A 25-question exam (84% to pass) and a one-to-one skills test. Pass both and your eCard is issued before 1.30pm.

Questions we get every week

Is the card accepted for my DHA licence?

Yes. The AHA BLS Provider eCard is accepted by DHA, DOH and MOHAP for licensing and renewal.

My card expired last month. Can I take the renewal?

Renewal is for cards that are still valid or expired within 30 days. Past that, book the full BLS Provider course.

What if I fail the skills test?

You retest on the day with your instructor. If you still need time, you join a later date free within 60 days.

Can I reschedule?

Free up to 48 hours before your session. Inside 48 hours there is a AED 75 transfer fee.

Where do I park?

Paid parking at Building 64, AED 10 per hour, or Healthcare City metro station is a six-minute walk.
