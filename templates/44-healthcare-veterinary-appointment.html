FAQ

Before you book

Are you taking on new pets?

Yes. We welcome new registrations daily. Bring any previous history and we will transfer records for you.

What if it is an emergency?

Call our 24-hour line and come straight in. We keep daily slots open for urgent cases and never turn away a crisis.

Do you offer payment plans?

Yes. We accept most pet insurers directly and offer instalment plans for larger procedures. Just ask at reception.

Can I see the same vet each time?

Wherever possible, yes. Continuity matters, so we try to match you with a familiar vet for every visit.
