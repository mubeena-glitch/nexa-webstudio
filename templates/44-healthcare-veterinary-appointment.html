Common questions

Do you see emergencies?

We keep two urgent slots open every day until 8pm. After that, call us and we will direct you to the nearest 24 hour hospital.

Do you take pet insurance?

We claim directly from PetSure and Oyster. For other insurers we give you the paperwork to claim yourself.

Can I get a home visit?

Yes, for vaccinations and end-of-life care within Jumeirah, Umm Suqeim and Al Barsha. Home visits start at AED 495.

Which animals do you treat?

Dogs, cats, rabbits and guinea pigs. For birds and reptiles we refer to a specialist colleague in Al Quoz.

Booster due, or something not right?

Most appointments are within three days.
