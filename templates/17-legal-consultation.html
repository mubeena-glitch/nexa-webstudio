Questions before booking

Is the consultation confidential?

Yes. Everything you share is confidential from the moment you contact us, whether or not you instruct us afterwards.

My employer is a big company. Does that matter?

We run a conflict check before confirming. If we act for your employer, we tell you the same day and suggest another firm.

How quickly can I be seen?

Usually within four working days. Termination matters with a deadline are seen within 48 hours.

Can I bring my spouse or a family member?

Yes, one person can join you. For family matters we will not meet both spouses together.

Do you take cases on a no-win, no-fee basis?

No. UAE rules limit contingency fees, and we prefer you know the full cost upfront.

Got a letter with a deadline?

Tell us the date in the form and we will see you within 48 hours.
