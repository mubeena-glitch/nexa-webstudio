What happens in each fortnight

Two live sessions a week, Tuesday and Thursday, 7pm to 8:30pm GST. Recordings are up by 10pm the same night.

Weeks 1 and 2: clean before you count

Spreadsheet structure, lookups, pivot tables and the habit of checking row counts. You clean 41,000 rows of real order data.

Weeks 3 and 4: SQL that answers a question

Joins, grouping, window functions. Every query is written against the same retail database so the logic compounds.

Weeks 5 and 6: Python for the repetitive parts

pandas for reshaping and charts you would put in front of a director. No machine learning detours.

Weeks 7 and 8: the written analysis

You pick a business question, answer it, and present in a 10-minute review with an instructor and two hiring managers.

Questions people ask before applying

How many hours a week does it take?

Three hours live, plus three to four hours on assignments. Most learners hold a full-time job.

Do I need to know any code?

No. You need to be comfortable in a spreadsheet. The application has a 15-minute task so we can check.

What if I miss a session?

Watch the recording the same night and post questions in the cohort channel. Instructors reply within one working day.

Is there a certificate?

Yes, issued only when your final analysis passes review. Attendance alone does not earn it.

Can my employer pay?

Yes. Choose employer-sponsored on the form and we send a quote with our trade licence for your finance team.
