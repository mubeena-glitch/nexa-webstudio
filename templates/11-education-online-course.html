FAQ

Before you enrol

How much time per week?

Two hours live, one office hour, two to four hours of project work. About six hours weekly.

What if I cannot attend a live session?

Every session is recorded. Office hours are also rotated, two slots per week to catch most time zones.

Do I get a certificate?

Yes. Issued on completion of the final project, not on attendance. The project, not the certificate, is what gets you hired.

What is your refund policy?

Full refund within the first two weeks if it is not for you. After that, partial refund pro-rated by remaining weeks.
