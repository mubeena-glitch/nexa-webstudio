Before you book

Is the call really free?

Yes. There is no charge and no obligation. About one in four callers become clients, and the rest leave with a clear list.

We already have an accountant. Is this still useful?

Often, yes. Bookkeepers keep the books. We check the tax position, which is a different job. Bring your accountant along.

We are in a free zone. Do we still need to register?

Yes. Every free zone entity must register for corporate tax, even if it qualifies for the 0% rate.

Can you file for us?

Yes. Corporate tax returns start at AED 4,750 and quarterly VAT returns at AED 1,150, quoted after the call.

Do you work outside Dubai?

Yes. We advise businesses across all seven emirates, by video or at your office.
