Questions before you enquire

Is there a fee to plan?

No. Planning and revisions are free. We are paid by hotels and airlines, and our prices match or beat booking them yourself.

Can you work with my Emirates Skywards miles?

Yes. We can price the trip with your flights booked on miles and the rest arranged by us.

What if a flight is cancelled while we are away?

You have a WhatsApp line to the office, staffed 24 hours while you travel. We rebook and move hotels if needed.

Do you arrange visas?

We tell you what you need and check the forms. Japan is visa-free for some passports and needs an application for others; we help with both.

How far ahead should I book?

Maldives over Eid and Christmas: 4 to 6 months. Japan in cherry blossom: 6 months. Otherwise 6 to 10 weeks is usually enough.
