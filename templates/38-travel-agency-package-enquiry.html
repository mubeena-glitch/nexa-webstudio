FAQ

Before you enquire

How does the planning process work?

You send us an enquiry with your ideas, a specialist calls you to understand the trip, and we return a tailored itinerary with costs. We refine it together until it feels right.

Is there a charge to plan a trip?

Designing your itinerary is free. You only pay once you are happy and ready to confirm, with a clear breakdown of every cost up front.

Are my flights and money protected?

Yes. All package holidays are fully financially protected, so your money and your trip are covered from the moment you book.

Can you arrange last-minute or complex trips?

We can. From a quiet long weekend to a multi-country honeymoon, simply tell us your timeframe in the enquiry and we will tell you honestly what is possible.
