Questions

Is my insurance accepted?

Choose your insurer on the form. If it is not on our direct billing list, we give you the paperwork to claim back.

I have not been to a dentist in years. Will I be judged?

No. About a third of our new patients have not seen a dentist in over five years.

Do you treat children?

Yes, from age 4. Children's check-ups are AED 250 self-pay.

Where do I park?

Free visitor parking behind the building, entrance from 25th Street. Mall of the Emirates metro is a 9-minute walk.

Toothache today? Call us

We keep 3 emergency slots open every day.
