Good first classes this week

Every class on the timetable is open to beginners. These four are where most people start.

Boxing foundations, Sunday and Tuesday 6:15am

Stance, guard, the jab and the cross. Slow enough to learn, hard enough to sweat.

Bag and burn, Monday and Wednesday 7:30pm

Ten three-minute rounds on the bag, then 12 minutes of conditioning. Our busiest class.

Pads with a partner, Thursday 6:45pm

Coach-led combinations on pads. You will swap partners twice, so come alone if you like.

Strength for fighters, Saturday 9am

Sled pushes, kettlebells and core. No gloves needed, good for rest days from running.

Before your first class

Do I need any experience?

No. About half of each class started with us as complete beginners.

What should I bring?

Trainers, water and a towel. We lend gloves and wraps for the free class. Showers and lockers are on site.

Is there sparring?

Only in the Friday technical sparring class, which needs a coach sign-off. Nobody spars in their first class.

Where do I park?

Free parking outside Warehouse 17. From Al Khail Road, take the Al Quoz 3 exit and turn at the second roundabout.

Do I need a card to book?

No. Just your name and number. If you cannot make it, cancel by WhatsApp so someone else gets the spot.
