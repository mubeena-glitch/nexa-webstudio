Before you book

How does the 12% saving work?

Find a lower public rate for the same room and dates, send us the link, and we beat it by 12%. The saving is applied before you pay.

What is the cancellation policy?

Flexible rates cancel free up to 72 hours before arrival. Advance purchase rates are 15% cheaper and non-refundable.

Can you arrange an airport transfer?

Yes. A car from DXB costs AED 210 each way and takes about 25 minutes outside rush hour. Add it when you book or reply to your confirmation email.

Are children welcome?

Yes. Under-sixes stay and eat breakfast free. The pool has a shallow end and lifeguards from 8am to 7pm.

Is there parking?

Free valet parking for hotel guests, with EV charging in the basement.
