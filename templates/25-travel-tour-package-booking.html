Why we cap groups at 11

Eleven people fit in one minibus, one guesthouse and around one table. Above that, a trip turns into logistics. Below it, you get the dinner invitations and detours that never make the brochure.

Before you book

Do I need visas?

UAE residents of most nationalities get visas on arrival in Oman, Jordan, Uzbekistan and Georgia. We check yours when you book.

How fit do I need to be?

Each trip has a grade. Jordan is the hardest: 4 days of 12 to 19 km on rough trails. Oman and Georgia are moderate.

Can I join from outside Dubai?

Yes. Book land only and meet the group at the first hotel. Prices drop by the flight cost.

Is travel insurance included?

No. It is required, and we can recommend a policy that covers trekking.
