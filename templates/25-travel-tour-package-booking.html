FAQ

Before you decide

How much is the deposit?

A refundable 15 per cent holds your place. The balance is due 45 days before departure.

Are flights included?

International flights are not included so you can use your own miles. We book every internal transfer.

What if I am travelling solo?

Most of our travellers are. No single supplement on shared rooms, or pay a small fee for your own.

Can you tailor a private trip?

Yes. Tell us the dates and the pace and we will build a private itinerary for your group.
