Before you book

Do I need a referral?

Not to see us as a self-pay patient. Some insurance plans need a GP referral first, and our coordinator will check yours when we call.

How much does an MRI cost?

A single-joint MRI is AED 1,650 self-pay. With insurance, it usually needs pre-approval, which we request for you on the day.

Can I choose my consultant?

Yes. Pick one in the form, or let us match you based on the problem.

Is there parking?

Free parking in the basement, with lift access to every floor.
