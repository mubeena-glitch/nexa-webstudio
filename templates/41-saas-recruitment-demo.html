Questions before the demo

What happens on the 25-minute demo?

Send us one open job description when you book. We set it up before the call, then walk you through posting, screening, scheduling and the offer stage on that role.

Where is candidate data stored?

In AWS Bahrain (me-south-1). We comply with the UAE PDPL and the Saudi PDPL, and every account gets a data processing agreement.

Does it connect to our HR system?

Growth and Scale sync hires to Bayzat, Zoho People, Darwinbox and SAP SuccessFactors. Anything else goes through our open API.

How long does it take to go live?

Most teams are live in 9 working days. We import your current candidates from a spreadsheet or your old system as part of onboarding.

Is there a contract?

Monthly plans cancel with 30 days notice. Annual plans are paid upfront and include free onboarding.
