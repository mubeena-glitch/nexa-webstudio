FAQ

Before you book

How long is the demo?

Twenty-five minutes. Send us an open role beforehand and we run it through the platform live, so you see your own hiring flow rather than a generic tour.

How is it priced?

Per active job, billed monthly with no per-recruiter fees. Volume pricing starts at twenty live roles. We confirm the figure on the demo.

Does it connect to our other tools?

Yes. Native links to the major job boards, calendars, email and the common HRIS platforms, plus an open API for anything bespoke.

How long does setup take?

Most teams import their open roles and go live within a week. We migrate your existing candidates and run a free onboarding session.
