FAQ

Before you tour

How long is a tour?

About twenty minutes. We show you the desks and offices that suit your team size, and you are welcome to stay and work the rest of the day on a free pass.

What does membership include?

Wifi, ethernet, meeting-room credits, barista coffee, printing, mail handling and member events. The only extras are additional meeting rooms beyond your credits.

Can I bring my team?

Yes. We have private offices from two to forty desks and can hold a space while you decide. Teams can scale up or down with thirty days notice.

What are the opening hours?

Reception is staffed from eight to seven on weekdays, and members have secure twenty-four-hour access every day of the week.
