Before you visit

How do I find you?

Warehouse 41 on Street 8A, Al Quoz Industrial Area 1. Turn in at the green gate. Parking is in our own yard, so no tickets.

Can we use the address on our trade licence?

Yes. Dedicated desk and private office members can register their licence here with Dubai Economy and Tourism. We provide the Ejari support documents.

What is the minimum term?

One month. Pay the first month and a deposit equal to one month, then leave with 30 days notice. Six month terms get 8% off.

Can I bring my dog?

Well-behaved dogs are welcome on the ground floor on Fridays. Not upstairs, and not in meeting rooms.

Is it noisy?

The open floor has acoustic panels and a quiet zone with a no-calls rule. Calls go in the booths or the yard.
