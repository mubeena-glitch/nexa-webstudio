Questions

Do you work on rented homes?

Yes. We focus on furniture, lighting, curtains and styling, with nothing that needs landlord approval.

What budget do I need?

Most whole-home projects spend AED 180,000 to 420,000 on furniture and works, on top of our fee.

Can we use furniture we already own?

Usually yes. On average clients keep about a third of what they have.

Do you work outside Dubai?

We take projects in Abu Dhabi and Ras Al Khaimah. Travel is charged at cost.
