What buyers ask before they reserve

Can non-residents buy here?

Yes. Al Reem Island is an investment zone, open to buyers of any nationality with full freehold title.

Is my money protected?

Every payment goes into an ADREC-regulated escrow account with Corniche Bank and is released only against certified construction progress.

Does a purchase qualify for a Golden Visa?

Purchases of AED 2,000,000 and above qualify you to apply for a 10-year Golden Visa once the SPA is registered.

What are the service charges?

Estimated at AED 16.80 per sq ft per year, confirmed by an ADREC-approved surveyor before handover.

Can I resell before handover?

Yes, once 40% of the price is paid. A transfer fee of AED 5,250 applies.
