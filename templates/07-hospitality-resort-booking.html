Before you book

Do UAE residents need a visa?

No. Every nationality gets a free 30-day visa on arrival in the Maldives with a confirmed booking and return ticket.

Can I book the villa without flights?

Yes. Villa-only rates start at AED 3,180 per night for two, half board, plus the seaplane at AED 2,640 per person return.

What is the deposit?

25% at booking, balance 45 days before travel. Free cancellation up to 60 days before arrival.

Is the resort good for children?

Family villas have fenced pools and the kids' club takes ages 4 to 11. Lagoon water villas are adults only.
