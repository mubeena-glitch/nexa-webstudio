Before your first visit

I have never lifted. Is this the wrong gym?

No. About 38% of new members are beginners. Your intro session covers the basics and the coaches check in on you.

Is there parking?

Yes, 62 free spaces in front of the building and more on the street behind.

Are there women-only hours?

A women-only coached class runs Sunday and Wednesday at 9am. The rest of the gym is mixed.

What happens after the 7 days?

Nothing, unless you choose a membership. We do not take card details for the trial.
