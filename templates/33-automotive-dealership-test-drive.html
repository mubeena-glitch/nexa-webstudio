Before you come in

Do I need a UAE licence?

Yes, or an international driving permit with your home licence if you are on a visit visa. We photocopy it before the drive.

Can you bring a car to my home?

For approved used cars, yes, anywhere in Dubai between Sunday and Thursday. We bring one car per visit.

Are approved used cars GCC spec?

Everything we sell is GCC spec with a full service history. Accident and service records are in the inspection report.

Can I return a car?

Approved used cars have a 7-day or 500 km exchange, whichever comes first, if anything is not as described.

Do you buy cars without selling me one?

Yes. We buy outright and transfer the money the same day the RTA transfer completes.
