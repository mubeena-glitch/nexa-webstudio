Before you book

Do I need a patch test?

Yes, for any colour if you have not coloured with us in the last six months. Pop in for five minutes at least 48 hours before.

What is your cancellation policy?

Free up to 24 hours before. Inside 24 hours we charge 50% of the booked service.

Is there parking?

Two shaded bays behind the salon, plus street parking on 2A Street. Valet is not available.

Can I bring my child?

Children over 6 can have a cut with Mireille on weekday afternoons, AED 120.
