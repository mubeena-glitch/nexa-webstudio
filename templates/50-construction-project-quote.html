FAQ

Before you request a quote

What do you need to quote a project?

Architectural drawings or a clear written brief, the site address and your target start date. If you only have a sketch, we can arrange a site visit to scope it properly.

How quickly will I get my quote?

For most projects we return a detailed, itemised quote within five working days of receiving your drawings. Larger schemes may need a site visit first.

Are you fully insured and accredited?

Yes. We hold ten million pounds public liability cover, are CHAS accredited and operate to ISO 9001 quality standards. Certificates are available on request.

Do you handle building control and warranties?

We manage building control sign-off and can arrange structural warranties on new builds. Your site manager coordinates inspections throughout the programme.
