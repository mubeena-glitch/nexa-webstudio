Before you send drawings

What do you need to quote?

Layout and reflected ceiling plans, a finishes schedule and the landlord fit-out guide. If you only have a layout, we can quote design and build.

Do you offer design and build?

Yes. Our in-house design team takes a project from test fit to approved drawings, usually in 4 to 6 weeks.

What is the smallest project you take on?

Around 1,500 sq ft or AED 450,000. For smaller works we can recommend a contractor we trust.

Are you insured?

Yes. Contractor's all risk and AED 10 million third-party liability on every project. Certificates go with the quote.

Can you work at night in an occupied building?

Yes. About a third of our projects run noisy works between 10pm and 6am to suit tenants and malls.
