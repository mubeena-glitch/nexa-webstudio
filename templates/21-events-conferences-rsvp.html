What the day is built around

Every session answers one question that retail ops leads asked us in last year's feedback survey.

What a delivery really costs

Line-by-line breakdown of a AED 23 home delivery in Dubai, from picking to the failed second attempt.

Returns without the margin hit

How a fashion marketplace cut its return rate from 31% to 22% in nine months, with the data.

Dark stores after the hype

Which quick-commerce sites in Riyadh and Dubai still make money, and which closed.

Twelve roundtables at lunch

Eight people per table, one topic each, chaired by a practitioner. Pick yours when you RSVP.
