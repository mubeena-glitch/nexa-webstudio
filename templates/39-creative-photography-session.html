How a session works

01

Book and plan

Choose a date and pay the deposit. A short call follows about what to wear and what the photos are for.

02

The session

Arrive 15 minutes early for a coffee. Noor works slowly and talks a lot; most people forget the camera by minute ten.

03

Choose your images

A proofing gallery of 80 to 140 frames arrives in 4 days. Pick your favourites.

04

Final gallery

Edited, full-resolution images ready to download in 9 working days, with prints if you ordered them.

Every session includes

  • A styling call and wardrobe guide before the shoot
  • Hair and make-up touch-ups for portrait and maternity sessions
  • Hand-edited colour and light, no heavy skin smoothing
  • A private online gallery for 12 months
  • A print release for personal use
  • AED 300 credit towards fine-art prints or albums

After the shoot

Before you book

When is the light best?

Between 9am and 12pm all year. From May to September we only shoot mornings because the afternoon light gets hard.

Can I reschedule?

Yes, free of charge up to 7 days before. Newborn sessions move with your baby's arrival at no cost.

Do you shoot outdoors in summer?

Only at sunrise, from 5.30am. Beach and desert sessions run October to April.

Can I bring our dog?

Yes, for family sessions. Tell us when you book so we can plan the time.

December mornings are nearly full

We shoot two sessions a day, so dates go quickly before the holidays.
