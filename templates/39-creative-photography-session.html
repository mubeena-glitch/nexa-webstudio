FAQ

Before you book

How long is a session?

Portrait and brand sessions run ninety minutes. Family sessions run two hours so younger children can settle. We never rush the clock.

Where do you shoot?

In the daylight studio or on location at a spot we agree together. Outdoor sessions are scheduled around the softest light of the day.

When will I see the photographs?

Your private online gallery arrives within ten working days. A handful of preview frames are sent within forty-eight hours.

What is the deposit and cancellation policy?

A deposit secures your date and comes off the final balance. Reschedule free of charge up to seven days before the session.
