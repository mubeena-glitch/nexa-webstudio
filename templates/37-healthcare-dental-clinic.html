Before your first visit

Can I use my insurance?

We bill Sahara Health, Harbourline, Crestline, Lifespring, CareLink and Northgate Health directly. Bring your card and Emirates ID; we check your cover before you sit down.

How soon can I be seen?

New patient visits are usually available within 4 working days. Emergencies are seen the same day if you call before 2pm.

Does the AED 395 offer apply with insurance?

It is a self-pay offer. If your insurance covers the visit, we bill them instead and you pay only your co-pay.

Is there parking?

Yes, seven bays behind the villa, entered from 19th Street.
