FAQ

Before you book

Are you taking on new patients?

Yes, we are open to new patients of all ages. Simply book a first appointment online and we will set up your records before you arrive.

Do you offer emergency appointments?

We keep same-day slots aside every day for pain, swelling, knocked-out teeth and broken fillings. Book online or call and we will fit you in.

How much does a check-up cost?

A standard examination is fixed-price and shown clearly at booking. Hygiene cleans and treatment plans are quoted in full before anything begins.

Can I spread the cost of treatment?

Yes. We offer interest-free monthly payment plans and an annual membership that covers your routine check-ups and cleans.
