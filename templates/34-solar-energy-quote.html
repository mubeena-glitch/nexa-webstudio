What villa owners ask

Do I need my community's permission?

Most communities ask for a no-objection certificate. We prepare the drawings and apply for it with Emaar, Nakheel, Dubai Properties or your owners association.

What happens to the power I do not use?

Under Shams Dubai it goes into the grid and DEWA credits it against future bills. Credits roll over month to month.

Will it work during a power cut?

A grid-tied system switches off for safety. If you want backup, add a battery and we wire it to your essential circuits.

Does sand and dust matter?

It cuts output by around 4 to 7% between cleans. A clean every 6 to 8 weeks keeps it in check; we offer a plan at AED 189 a visit.

I am renting. Can I still install?

Only with written landlord approval, since the DEWA account and the roof belong to the owner.
