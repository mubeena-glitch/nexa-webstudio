How Fils works on payday

Connect your bank

Read-only access through the UAE Open Finance framework. Fils can see transactions. It cannot move a single dirham.

Tell it your fixed costs once

Rent cheques, DEWA, Salik top-ups, school fees by term, the car loan. Fils spots most of them from your history.

Payday sorts itself

When salary lands, Fils earmarks bills and your savings target first, then spreads what is left across the days until next payday.

One number each morning

A 7am notification with what is safe to spend today. Overspend on Thursday and Friday adjusts quietly.

Built for how money moves here

Budgeting apps built in London or San Francisco do not understand quarterly rent cheques, a 13th month school fee or the annual flight home. Fils was built in Dubai for exactly that.

  • Rent paid in 1, 2, 4 or 12 cheques
  • Sinking pots for annual flights, visa renewal and car registration
  • Remittance tracking for money sent home
  • Works with salaries in AED, plus USD and GBP accounts

Before you join

Which banks will work at launch?

Corniche Bank, Falcon Bank, Capital Bay Bank, Dune Commercial Bank, Creekside Islamic Bank, Northern Bank and Lumen Digital Bank. More follow as they join Open Finance.

Can Fils move my money?

No. The connection is read-only. We cannot make payments, transfers or changes to your account.

Where is my data kept?

On servers in the UAE, encrypted at rest and in transit. We do not sell data and we do not show ads.

I am paid irregularly. Will it work?

Yes. Freelancers set a monthly pay figure and Fils smooths income across the month from what actually arrives.

When will I get in?
