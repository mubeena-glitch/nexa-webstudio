Questions parents ask on tours

Do you have places for January 2027?

Yes, in Saplings and Explorers. Seedlings has two places from February. Pathfinders has a waiting list of seven.

Can I pay monthly?

Yes. Termly fees can be split into monthly direct debits at no extra cost.

What happens if my child is unwell?

We ask that children stay home for 48 hours after a temperature or sickness. Our nurse, Joy Fernandes, is on site every day from 8am to 4pm.

Which schools do children move on to?

Most go on to British-curriculum primary schools within 15 minutes of the nursery, in Umm Suqeim, Al Barsha and Al Safa.

Is there parking?

There is a drop-off lane on 17A Street with staff at the car door from 7.30am to 8.30am.
