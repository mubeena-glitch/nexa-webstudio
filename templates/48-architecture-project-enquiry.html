What we design

New houses

Infill plots, replacement dwellings and self-build homes, typically 140 to 420 square metres.

Extensions and refurbishments

Small commercial buildings

How a project runs

01

Feasibility study, 3 to 5 weeks

Measured survey, planning history, three options and a cost range from our quantity surveyor. Fixed fee from £1,850 to £3,400.

02

Concept and planning, 3 to 6 months

One design developed with you, pre-application advice where useful, and the planning submission.

03

Technical design and tender

Construction drawings and specification, then a competitive tender to three or four builders.

04

On site

Fortnightly site visits, valuations and the snagging list, until the final certificate.

Questions we are asked first

What will your fees be?

After feasibility, full services are usually 11% to 14% of construction cost for houses and extensions. We quote a fixed fee per stage, not a percentage that rises with the budget.

What budget do I need?

Our smallest projects are rear extensions from about £180,000 including VAT. New houses typically start around £650,000 build cost.

Do you work outside London?

Yes, across Kent, Surrey and East Sussex, if the site is within two hours of the studio by train.

Can we use our own builder?

Yes. We will still produce a full tender package so their price is checked against the drawings.

When could you start?

We currently have space for three new feasibility studies starting in January 2027.
