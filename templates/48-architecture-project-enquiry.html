FAQ

Common questions before you enquire

What does it cost to get started?

We begin with a fixed-price feasibility study, typically between one and three thousand pounds depending on scale. It is credited against your project fee if you proceed to full design.

Do you handle planning permission?

Yes. We prepare and submit the planning application, manage council correspondence and represent you through any conditions or appeals.

How long does a project take?

A domestic extension usually runs eight to fourteen months from brief to completion, including planning and construction. We give you a realistic programme at feasibility stage.

Can you work with my builder?

Of course. We are happy to design for a contractor you trust, or to run a competitive tender and recommend firms we have worked with before.

Tell us about your site.

Share your brief and we will arrange a project consultation within two working days.
