Questions

Do you charge a fee?

No. We are paid a commission by the insurer you choose. It does not change your premium.

I have a claim in the last year. Can I still get cover?

Yes. Expect a higher price for 12 months. We will tell you which insurers treat claims most fairly.

My licence is less than a year old.

Several insurers cover new drivers, usually with a higher excess. Choose that option in step two.

Can I keep my no-claims discount?

Yes. Bring a no-claims letter from your current insurer, or a home-country letter less than 12 months old.

Are you licensed?

Yes. Rayah Motor Brokers is licensed by the Central Bank of the UAE, licence number 236.
