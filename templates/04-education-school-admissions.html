FAQ

Before you visit

What is the admissions process?

Campus visit, family interview, child assessment. Decisions issued within two weeks of the assessment.

Do you offer sibling discounts?

Yes. 10 per cent off the second child, 15 per cent off the third and beyond. Applies to active enrolments.

What is your transport coverage?

Door-to-door coach service covers the city and most surrounding areas. Route map shared after enrolment.

Are scholarships available?

Academic, sports, and arts scholarships offered for Year 7 and Year 12 entry. Up to 50 per cent of fees.
