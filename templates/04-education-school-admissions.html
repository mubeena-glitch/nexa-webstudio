How admission works

Most families have a decision within 12 working days of applying.

01

Visit

Come to an open morning or book a private tour with our Registrar.

02

Apply online

Submit the form with the last two school reports and a AED 525 application fee.

03

Assessment

FS1 to Year 1: a relaxed play session. Year 2 and above: CAT4 and a short English and maths task, around 90 minutes.

04

Offer and seat deposit

Offers are emailed within five working days. A AED 5,000 deposit, deducted from term one fees, secures the place.
