FAQ

Before you sponsor

How is 100% of my donation reaching the child?

Our operating costs are funded separately by a small board of long-term donors. Your donation funds programmes only. Audited annually, accounts public.

What is the commitment?

Annual sponsorship of AED 1,800 per child. Renew or stop annually. No multi-year lock.

Can I visit the school?

Yes. Annual partner visits arranged twice a year. Sponsors welcome with one month's notice.

Is my donation tax-deductible?

UK and US donors receive Gift Aid / 501(c)(3) receipts. UAE donors receive a board-issued acknowledgement.
