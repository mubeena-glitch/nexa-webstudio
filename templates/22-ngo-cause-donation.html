Questions donors ask

Can I claim Gift Aid?

Yes, if you pay UK income tax. Tick the box on the form and we claim an extra 25p for every £1 from HMRC.

Can I cancel a monthly gift?

Any time, by replying to any of our emails or cancelling the Direct Debit with your bank. No call, no questions.

Do you fund anything other than cataracts?

No. One operation, one hospital. It keeps costs low and lets us check every result.

Can I give from outside the UK?

Yes, by card. Gift Aid only applies to UK taxpayers.

Will you share my details?

Never. We do not sell, swap or rent our donor list.
