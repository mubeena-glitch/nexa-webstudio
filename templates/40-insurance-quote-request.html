Questions HR and finance teams ask

What is the minimum company size?

Five employees for most insurers, and two for some Dubai basic plans. Below that, we arrange individual policies.

Do you charge a fee?

No. We are paid commission by the insurer, which is already in the premium you would pay going direct. We disclose it in the quote.

Are pre-existing conditions covered?

On group plans with 10 or more staff, usually yes, from day one. Under 10, some insurers ask for medical declarations and may apply a waiting period.

Can we cover some staff differently?

Yes. Most of our clients have two or three tiers by grade, for example comprehensive for managers and enhanced for everyone else.

Are you regulated?

Yes. Saffar Benefits is licensed by the Central Bank of the UAE as an insurance broker.
