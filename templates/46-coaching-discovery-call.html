Before you book

Is the discovery call really free?

Yes. The 30-minute call is free with no obligation. It is a chance for us both to see if working together makes sense.

How are sessions delivered?

Sessions run over video call, so you can join from anywhere. Evening slots are available for those who work full time.

How long is a coaching engagement?

Most clients work in blocks of six sessions over three months, though some stay longer. There is no lock-in.

What kind of coaching is this?

Career and leadership coaching for people who want direction, confidence, and a plan they will actually follow through.
