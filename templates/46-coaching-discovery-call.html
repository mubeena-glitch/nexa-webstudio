We test the move before you make it

Most career changers spend a year thinking and a weekend deciding. We do the opposite. Within the first month you speak to people already doing the work you are considering, and you try a small piece of it yourself.

  • Your transferable skills mapped against three target roles
  • At least six conversations with people in those fields, set up with our help
  • A salary floor and runway figure you have agreed with your household
  • A CV and LinkedIn rewrite for the new direction, not the old one

The 16-week programme

Ten one-hour sessions, spaced so there is work to do in between.

01

Weeks 1 to 3: take stock

Strengths, values and money. We look hard at what you would miss and what you would not.

02

Weeks 4 to 8: test three options

Informational interviews, a small paid or volunteer project, and an honest look at the numbers for each.

03

Weeks 9 to 12: choose and prepare

One direction, a story that explains the change, and the qualifications or portfolio it needs.

04

Weeks 13 to 16: make the approach

Applications, introductions and interview practice, or a launch plan if you are going independent.

Before you book

Is the call a sales pitch?

No. We spend 40 minutes on your situation. If coaching is not the right next step, we will say so and point you somewhere more useful.

Do you find me a job?

No. We are not recruiters. We help you choose a direction and approach it well, and we make introductions where we can.

Can sessions be online?

Yes. About 60% of clients work with us on video, including people in Abu Dhabi, Riyadh and Doha.

I am on an employment visa. Is changing career risky?

It needs planning, which is part of the programme. We cover notice periods, visa timing and end of service benefits in weeks 1 to 3.
